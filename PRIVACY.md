# OoFind privacy policy

OoFind does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- It runs only when you click its toolbar button or press its shortcut, and only in that tab.
- It talks only to the Odoo server of the page you opened it on, with your existing session, so
  it sees exactly what your Odoo user can see.
- Its settings (panel width, pinned fields, the history of filters you applied with OoFind) are
  stored locally in your browser with `storage.local`. They contain domains (filter conditions),
  not record data.
- Favorites you save are stored in your Odoo database as regular Odoo favorites.
- There is no analytics, telemetry, remote code or third-party service.
